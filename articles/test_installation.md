# 3-Chapter Plan: The Impact of Generative AI on Assessment Practices in Higher Education

## Chapter 1 — Introduction and Landscape

**Research Question:** How is generative AI reshaping assessment design and practices in higher education?

**Key Themes:**
- Overview of generative AI tools adopted by students and faculty (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot)
- Typology of assessment formats and their varying vulnerabilities to AI-assisted completion
- Emergence of "AI-resilient" assessment design as a strategic response

**Expected Contribution:** Establishes the scope of the problem and maps the current institutional landscape across disciplines.

---

## Chapter 2 — Critical Analysis of Assessment Practices

**Research Question:** What assessment strategies are proving effective in maintaining academic integrity while leveraging AI as a pedagogical tool?

**Key Themes:**
- Comparative review of traditional vs. redesigned assessments (oral defences, process-based portfolios, reflective journals)
- Institutional policy responses: detection-first vs. integration-first approaches
- Student and faculty perspectives on fairness and learning outcomes

**Expected Contribution:** Provides an evidence-based taxonomy of response strategies and their documented outcomes.

---

## Chapter 3 — Toward an Integrated Framework

**Research Question:** What principles should guide the co-design of assessment practices that embrace generative AI while preserving learning integrity?

**Key Themes:**
- Synthesis of effective design principles emerging from the literature
- Ethical and equity dimensions (digital divide, disciplinary differences)
- A proposed framework: "Authentic AI-Integrated Assessment" (AAIA)

**Expected Contribution:** Delivers an actionable framework for curriculum and assessment designers adapting to an AI-augmented academic environment.
